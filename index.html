About Dr. Bonny Ntare

Dr. Bonny Ntare is a renowned agricultural scientist known for significant contributions to international agricultural research for development. He actively continues to inspire and guide the next generation of agricultural scientists.

Achievements

  • Issued the Researcher and Manager Certificate by ICRISAT in November 2013 for Outstanding Contribution towards the mission of ICRISAT in the Semi-Arid Tropics of Sub-Saharan Africa
  • Received a Certificate of Recognition from the Government of Mali in March 2009 for Outstanding contribution to Agricultural Research for Development in Mali

Legacy

Dr. Bonny Ntare's work and dedication to agricultural research and development have had a profound impact on the landscape of international agricultural science. His legacy continues to inspire and guide the next generation of agricultural scientists.
